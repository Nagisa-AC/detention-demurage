How free-time days are counted. The rules do not settle whether the day a container becomes available counts as the first free day. This report counts it, which gives the lower amount. Otherwise finding 03 is 750.00 and the total 3,250.00.

Method

Each invoice was checked for the information the federal billing rules require, invoice timing, charges during free time, arithmetic, and days billed twice. Every value used was read from the invoice and confirmed by a reviewer against the page before any check ran.

Limitations

These amounts are claims. The carrier may accept, reduce or reject them, and the amount recovered may differ. This report is not legal advice.

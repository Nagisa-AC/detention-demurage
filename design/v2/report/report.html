Method

Each invoice was checked for the information the federal billing rules require, the timing of the invoice, charges during free time, arithmetic, and days billed more than once. Every value used was read from the invoice and confirmed by a reviewer against the page before any check was applied. Findings rely only on what is printed on the invoices and on terms you have provided.

How free-time days are counted

The rules do not settle whether the day a container becomes available counts as the first day of free time. This report counts it, which gives the lower amount. If it is not counted, Finding 3 rises from 500.00 to 750.00 and the total from 3,000.00 to 3,250.00.

Limitations
